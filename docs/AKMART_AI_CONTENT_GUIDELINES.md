## 1. CONTENT TONES & BRAND VOICE

1. **Professional**: Default authoritative e-commerce product tone. Focuses on technical accuracy, engineering excellence, and reliability.
2. **Premium**: Elevated luxury tone for flagship electronics and high-ticket items.
3. **Promotional**: Urgent, benefit-driven copy for flash sales, holiday discounts, and limited-time coupons.
4. **Friendly**: Conversational, approachable tone for customer shopping assistance and FAQ replies.

---

## 2. STRICT TRUTH-IN-ADVERTISING LAWS

- **No Fabricated Specifications**: Battery size, RAM, processor, and warranty must strictly match database records.
- **No False Claims**: AI must never claim "Doctor recommended", "100% cure", or unsupported medical benefits.
- **Multilingual Integrity**: Brand names (*Apple*, *Samsung*, *Nike*) and technical numbers must never be corrupted across English, Malayalam, Hindi, Arabic, French, and German.
