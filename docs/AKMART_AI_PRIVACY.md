**Date**: August 2026  

---

## 1. CORE PRIVACY LAWS

1. **Customer Data Isolation**: Customer A cannot view Customer B's orders, wallet balances, loyalty points, or wishlist items.
2. **Zero Sensitive Profiling**: Segmentation and intelligence models rely exclusively on non-sensitive commercial metrics (spend, orders, return count, frequency).
3. **No Unilateral Decisions**: AI never blocks users, denies returns, or docks loyalty balances autonomously.
4. **Data Masking**: Credit card numbers, API keys, passwords, and sensitive PII are stripped before LLM synthesis.
