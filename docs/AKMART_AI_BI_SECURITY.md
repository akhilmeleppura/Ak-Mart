## 1. FINANCIAL REPORTING PRIVACY & SAFETY

1. **Role Gating**: Net profit, margin percentages, and cost breakdowns are strictly restricted to `super_admin` and `finance` roles.
2. **Zero Financial Hallucination**: AI must refuse to speculate on unrecorded revenue or missing ledger data.
3. **Strict Read-Only Guarantee**: The BI system cannot insert, update, or delete financial records.
