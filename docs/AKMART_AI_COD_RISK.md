## 1. COD POLICY RULES

Customers with $\ge 3$ COD orders exhibiting $\ge 50\%$ cancellations or Return-to-Origin (RTO) are flagged with `Require Prepaid Payment`. Legitimate first-time or low-risk buyers are granted full COD access.
