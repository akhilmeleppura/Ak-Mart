## 1. MOVEMENT ANOMALY DETECTION THRESHOLDS

- **Large Manual Adjustments**: Adjustments exceeding $\ge 50$ units trigger automated audit logging.
- **Negative Shrinkage Variances**: Discrepancies between expected book stock and physical cycle count $> 5\%$ are flagged for management review.
- **Non-Accusatory Rule**: Discrepancy reports flag system variances without automated employee accusations.
