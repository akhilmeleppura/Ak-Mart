## 1. DAILY BUSINESS BRIEF SPECIFICATION

Generates real-time daily operational briefs covering:
- **Executive Summary**: High-level daily top-line overview.
- **Sales & Conversion**: Gross revenue, order volume, and AOV.
- **Profit & Margin**: Estimated net profit and operating margins.
- **Customer Acquisitions**: New registrations and repeat purchasers.
- **Inventory Runway**: Low-stock SKUs ($\le 5$ units) and out-of-stock items.
- **Actionable Recommendations**: Clear supply chain and marketing action points.
