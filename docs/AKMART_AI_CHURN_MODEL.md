## 1. EXPLAINABLE CHURN RISK SIGNALS

| Churn Risk Level | Deterministic Signal Trigger | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Risk** | No purchase activity in $\ge 90$ days for a previous buyer, or Return Rate $> 30\%$. | Deploy Win-Back Email / WhatsApp Campaign with promo incentive. |
| **Medium Risk** | Purchase gap $\ge 45$ days (exceeds customer's normal repurchase cycle). | Re-engage with category new arrivals and bundle recommendations. |
| **Low Risk** | Active purchases within the last 45 days. | Regular lifecycle nurturing. |

---

## 2. NON-DISCRIMINATIVE SAFETY LAW

AI churn prediction is an advisory recommendation for marketing teams. Under no circumstances does a churn score trigger automatic account restriction or penalty.
