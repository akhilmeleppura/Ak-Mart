# 🔒 AKMART AI — MARKETING SECURITY & CONSENT POLICY

**Document ID**: AKMART-DOC-MKT-SEC-005  
**Date**: August 2026  

---

## 1. MARKETING GUARDRAILS & MANDATORY LAWS

1. **Human-in-the-Loop Approval**: AI cannot trigger mass dispatch of emails, WhatsApp messages, or SMS without explicit admin authorization.
2. **Consent & Opt-Out Enforcement**: Unsubscribed users are excluded from all campaign targets.
3. **No 100% Discount Coupons**: Coupon suggestions are bounded by margin and maximum discount limits.
4. **PII Masking**: Customer emails and phone numbers are excluded from marketing prompt context.
