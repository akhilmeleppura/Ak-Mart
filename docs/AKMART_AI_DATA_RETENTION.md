## 1. RETENTION SCHEDULES

- **Customer Chat Sessions**: Retained for 30 days, then automatically purged.
- **Admin Observability & Security Audit Logs**: Retained for 90 days in compliance with security audit mandates.
- **Customer Right-to-be-Forgotten**: When a customer account deletion is triggered, all associated AI session interactions are purged immediately.
