## 1. ETHICAL RISK ASSESSMENT LAWS

1. **Strictly Non-Demographic**: Risk scoring evaluates only commercial metrics (AOV deviations, payment failures, RTO rates). Race, religion, ethnicity, gender, and nationality are strictly prohibited.
2. **Human-in-the-Loop Decisions**: No customer is banned or order cancelled autonomously by AI; high-risk orders are placed in the manual review queue for human judgment.
