## 1. EXPLAINABLE CUSTOMER LIFECYCLE SEGMENTS

| Segment Name | Criteria & Business Rule | Description & Next Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VIP** | Lifetime Spend $\ge \$1,000$ OR Completed Orders $\ge 10$. | Highest tier; eligible for exclusive preview, dedicated concierge, and VIP loyalty rewards. |
| **High Value** | Lifetime Spend $\ge \$500$. | Key revenue contributor; nurture with category cross-sells. |
| **Returning Customer** | Completed Orders $\ge 2$ AND Last Order $\le 90$ days. | Active repeat buyer; maintain engagement with new arrival notifications. |
| **New Customer** | Completed Orders $= 1$ OR Registered within 30 days. | Recent acquisition; engage with welcome series and review requests. |
| **At Risk** | Completed Orders $\ge 2$ AND Last Order $> 90$ days. | Dormant repeat buyer; trigger win-back coupon campaign. |
| **Inactive** | Completed Orders $= 0$ in $> 120$ days OR Last Order $> 180$ days. | Inactive account; re-engagement candidate. |

---

## 2. PRIVACY & ETHICAL BOUNDARIES

- **Zero Sensitive Attributes**: Segmentation relies purely on commercial transaction metrics (frequency, recency, monetary value, return volume).
- **No Exclusionary Profiling**: No racial, religious, health, political, or demographic markers are ever stored or processed.
